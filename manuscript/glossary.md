# Glossary

Entirely skippable, but posted here if readers would appreciate refreshers for the admittedly many unique terms in the book.

- Arcane: Eyael’s orthodox Magic system.

- Arcane Creature: feral biological organisms naturally constituted from Aether by Worldhearts, innately attuned to the Arcane and its Magics, and a minor constituent of Eyael’s ecosystems.

- Arcane Tongue: the Arcane’s mystical language intelligible only to Arcanists.

- Arcanist: a person with a soul attuned to the Arcane and thus its practitioner.

- Aether: the primordial invisible energy that permeates Eyael, the building block of energy and matter, and the environmental medium necessary to manifest Magical powers.

- Aetheric Creatures: feral biological organisms naturally constituted from Aether by Worldhearts, empowered at higher Tiers with primordial elemental magic, and the main constituent of Eyael’s ecosystems.

- Alfainia: an island-nation offshore the Nordic continent.

- Beastman: sapient but primal biological organisms naturally constituted from Aether by Worldhearts, innately attuned to the Arcane and its Magics, and the smallest constituent of Eyael’s ecosystems.

- Calling: the specific role an Arcanist or Profaner attains at a specific Magic Branch and Tier that defines what powers they can wield and what they must do to Self-Actualize.

- Calling-specific feat: the specific act an Arcanist or Profaner must accomplish at certain Tiers to progress to the next Tier.

- Creator: one of the deities in Eyael’s pantheon of gods.

- Demigod: an Arcanist or Profaner who has reached a high enough Tier to attain divinity.

- Dwarves: short, humanoids with human features and a lack of biological pigmentation as part of their subterranean evolution.

- Elves: human-sized humanoids with human features but pointed ears, grey skin, and eye and hair pigmentation whose color reflects innate individual Magical affinity.

- Essence: an amorphous, gelatinous, indestructible organ in Arcane Creatures, Beastmen, and Profane Creatures that contains the host-creature’s innate Magic.

- Evania: an island offshore the Nordic continent and Alfainia’s core homeland.

- Eyael: the world.

- Father: one of the deities in Eyael’s pantheon of gods.

- God of War: one of the deities in Eyael’s pantheon of gods.

- Humans: self-explanatory.

- Magic Artifact: an instrument forged with an Essence and/or Magic Gland that can cast the donor-organ’s innate Magic.

- Magic Charm: a charm imbued with the power of extremely powerful Arcanists or Profaners that functions as a single-use Magic Artifact.

- Magic Gland: a gelatinous organ in Arcane Creatures that lets them cast Magic.

- Norden: the northernmost continent in the world of Eyael.

- Nordnir: Evania island’s northern region: predominantly plateaus and mountains.

- Ostnir: Evania island’s eastern region, predominantly flatlands.

- Pértus: the central and largest continent in the world of Eyael.

- Portsmouth: Alfainia’s capital.

- Profane: Eyael’s demonic, unorthodox Magic system.

- Profane Creature: feral biological organisms naturally constituted from Aether during Profane Floods, innately attuned to the Profane and its Magics, and an aberrant category of invasive creatures in Eyael’s ecosystems.

- Profane Floods: cyclical, recurrent worldwide catastrophes that inundate the world in Aether and spawn millions upon billions of Profane Creatures.

- Profane Tongue: the Profane’s mystical language intelligible only to Profaners.

- Profaner: a person with a soul attuned to the Profane and thus its practitioner.

- Sarh: a continental empire in the Pértusian continent and one of Eyael’s superpowers.

- Self-actualization: a lived, experiential process where an Arcanist or Profaner must embody and live the ideals of their Calling to progress it and thus ascend to the next Tier’s Calling.

- Spawning: the Aetheric phenomena wherein a Worldheart’s Aether coalesces into an Aetheric Creature, Arcane Creature, or Beastman.

- Tier: the discrete levels of power for Aetheric Creatures, Arcane Creatures, Arcanists, Beastmen, Profane Creatures, and Profaners.

- Windbury: a major Alfainian city and Ostnir’s provincial capital.

- Worldheart: Naturally-occurring, recurrent, spontaneous natural phenomena where atmospheric Aether converges into a sky-piercing pillar of light that spawns Aetheric Creatures, Arcane Creatures, Beastmen, and natural resources.

- Vastnir: Evania island’s western region, predominantly hills and bays.
