# Locations

Compiled from a full sequential read of Draft Zero. See `manuscript/glossary.md` for the author's
own in-text glossary entries (reproduced verbatim); entries below add chapter-sourced context the
glossary itself doesn't spell out.

## The world

- **Eyael** — the world the story is set in (glossary).
- **Pértus** — the central and largest continent; home to **Sarh**, a continental empire and one of
  Eyael's superpowers, and worshippers of the God of War (glossary; Ch1 Scripture references Sarh's
  "priest-cum-diplomats").
- **Norden** — the northernmost continent (glossary); the ongoing war is explicitly framed as being
  fought to conquer "the Creator's remaining territory on the mainland," i.e., Imperium Divinus's
  holdings on Norden (Ch1, Ch5).
- **Evania** — an island offshore the Nordic continent; Alfainia's core homeland (glossary). Divided
  into three regions:
  - **Nordnir** — northern region, plateaus and mountains.
  - **Ostnir** — eastern region, flatlands; contains Windbury.
  - **Vastnir** — western region, hills and bays; contains Portsmouth (per Marelyn's telegram
    address in Ch3: "Vastnir Province").

## Alfainia (the nation)

- Island-nation, formerly occupied by Imperium Divinus; liberated roughly 74 years before the
  novella's present (Ch1 reference to "seventy-four-years ago"); named after its liberator, **Saint
  Alfred** (Ch4).
- A constitutional Commonwealth with Parliament, multiple parties (Labor, Trade, Dominions, People,
  Freedom, Justice — named in Ch2 by a disillusioned Windbury commuter), and overseas **Dominions**
  on "all seven continents" (Ch2) whose continued deference to Alfainian leadership is explicitly
  framed by Edward Cook as the geopolitical linchpin holding the Commonwealth together (Ch5).
- Bound by an "ancient" ritual compact between Mankind (the **Heralds of Freedom**) and Dwarfkind
  (the **Pillars of Iron**), renewed at each change of government (Ch1).
- Politically and religiously entangled with Sarh/the God of War since liberation — a relationship
  Marelyn regards as having mortgaged Alfainia's sovereignty; Charles Winston, by contrast, advocates
  returning to the Creator's empire (Ch4).

### Portsmouth
- Alfainia's capital (glossary); seat of Parliament and the Commonwealth Exchequer.
- Bisected by the **Rorsch River**, grey and busy, overlooked by a Parliament-building balcony in
  Ch1.
- **Southern Portsmouth** — home to Government and "the affluent" (Ch7).
- **Northern Portsmouth** — home to "a vast majority" of the city's residents: factories, flats,
  foundries, warehouses; site of **Saint Alfred's Cathedral**, across the Rorsch from the
  Government District (Ch6–7).
- **Excelsior Avenue** — financial district street; location of the Stock Exchange Marelyn
  frequents and the newsstands where the Imperial incursion headlines break (Ch5).
- **Government District** — includes the Commonwealth Exchequer (Carden's workplace) and the
  Imperial District nearby, whose historic spires are described as monuments to Imperium Divinus's
  nine-year occupation (Ch5).
- **Prudence Street** — colloquial name for Parliament's locale (Ch2: "squatting in Prudence
  Street"), analogous to a real-world "Downing Street."
- **Wheatfield's Bistro** — Carden's favored upscale restaurant.

### Windbury
- A major Alfainian city, Ostnir's provincial capital (glossary); Carden's and Horace's hometown,
  and the Winston and Baker families' home city.
- **Windbury Train Station** / **Central Square** — site of the Deliverance Day riot's aftermath
  (black-shrouded corpses, Ch3) and the Government District Telegram Office.
- **Windbury Cathedral** — across Central Square from the train station; runs a soup kitchen staffed
  by lay clergy; its
  bell tower (tallest point in the city) is used as an observation post during the Ch3 Imperial
  flotilla standoff; its crypt doubles as a bomb shelter.
- **Oakmoore estate** — Julian and Jane's expansive marble manor; features manicured
  grounds, Aetheric-Creature topiaries, and Imperial iconography subtly worked into the architecture
  (Ch4).
- **Winston Manor** — Charles and Beatrice's "modernist" manor, only fifteen years old, in downtown
  Windbury (Ch4).
- **Bakers For You** — Annie and Roger's pastry factory and soup kitchen (Ch2).
- Suffers severe, visible economic decline across the novella: shuttered shops, unrepaired riot
  damage, lengthening soup-kitchen lines (Ch2, Ch6, Ch8) — contrasted with Portsmouth's comparative
  insulation until the Ch5 air incursions begin.
- A grassy hill countryside spot outside the city, Carden and Annie's old teenage retreat, becomes
  Carden and Marelyn's own in Ch8.

### Other named settlements (Dominions/provinces mentioned in passing)
- Aberclair, Glenfold, Pontmyr — named as sites of Imperial air-fleet sightings (Ch5 newspaper
  headlines) and, for Aberclair/Pontmyr, as sites of tax-exemption and revenue disputes Edward
  references (Ch6).

## The war's geography
- Imperial (Imperium Divinus / the Creator's) naval and air forces repeatedly probe Alfainian and
  Dominion waters at "extreme distance," using Atmospheric Magic-conjured storms to screen their
  withdrawals (Ch3, Ch5) — a pattern of escalating brinksmanship rather than open war until the
  novella's end.
- Sarh maintains a forward aerial-fleet presence shadowing Imperial incursions near Alfainia (the
  Eighteenth Aerial Fleet, Ch3), consistent with Alfainia's role as a "forward-operating base" for
  the God of War's war effort (Carden's words, Ch1).
