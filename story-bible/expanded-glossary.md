# Expanded Glossary

The author's own glossary (`manuscript/glossary.md`) is reproduced there verbatim and unedited, as
written. This document adds, for each entry, where it is actually used or shown in the manuscript's
narrative chapters — distinguishing terms the prose puts to active use from terms that (in this
Draft Zero) appear only in the glossary itself, defined but not yet dramatized.

Entries are alphabetical, matching the source glossary's order.

---

**Arcane** — Eyael's orthodox Magic system.
→ Underpins the entire plot's magic from Ch1 onward (Arcane Tongue, Arcanists, Zeal/Law/Armaments/
Shadow/Mitotic/Atmospheric/Gravitational/Space/Empowerment/Enchanting Magic are all Arcane
disciplines in use on-page). See `magic-system.md`.

**Arcane Creature** — feral biological organisms naturally constituted from Aether by Worldhearts,
innately attuned to the Arcane and its Magics, and a minor constituent of Eyael's ecosystems.
→ Not directly depicted on-page in the read chapters; no named Arcane Creature appears. (Ch4's
estate topiaries are sculpted into the likeness of "Aetheric Creatures," a related but distinct
category — see below.)

**Arcane Tongue** — the Arcane's mystical language intelligible only to Arcanists.
→ Actively plot-critical: self-authenticates the God of War's and the Creator's *Scriptures* (Ch1,
Ch5); forces truth under Horace's Law Magic interrogation (Ch3); used deliberately by Carden and
Julian to force mutual honesty in their Ch5 confrontation; used by Carden to signal sincerity when
conveying classified information to Roger (Ch8) and accepting termination from Edward (Ch7).

**Arcanist** — a person with a soul attuned to the Arcane and thus its practitioner.
→ Confirmed Arcanists on-page: Carden Oakmoore, Julian Oakmoore, Edward Cook, Roger Baker, Horace
Livingstone, Renee Rose. See `characters.md` for each.

**Aether** — the primordial invisible energy that permeates Eyael, the building block of energy and
matter, and the environmental medium necessary to manifest Magical powers.
→ Shown as something Arcanists can feel disturbed by one another's presence/Magic: Edward's
Arcanist nature "stirs the air's ever-pervasive, invisible Aether" (Ch1); Carden senses Renee's Zeal
Magic in the Ch2 crowd before seeing her; "distant Aetheric fluctuations" ripple the air at the end
of Ch2.

**Aetheric Creatures** — feral biological organisms naturally constituted from Aether by
Worldhearts, empowered at higher Tiers with primordial elemental magic, and the main constituent of
Eyael's ecosystems.
→ Appear only as a decorative motif in the read chapters: the Oakmoore estate's grounds feature
"massive topiaries of fierce Aetheric Creatures" (Ch4). Also referenced generically as what
concrete Commune palisades are built to defend against, alongside Arcane Beasts (Ch2).

**Alfainia** — an island-nation offshore the Nordic continent.
→ The novella's primary setting throughout; see `locations.md`.

**Beastman** — sapient but primal biological organisms naturally constituted from Aether by
Worldhearts, innately attuned to the Arcane and its Magics, and the smallest constituent of Eyael's
ecosystems.
→ Not depicted on-page in the read chapters.

**Calling** — the specific role an Arcanist or Profaner attains at a specific Magic Branch and Tier
that defines what powers they can wield and what they must do to Self-Actualize.
→ Never named explicitly as "a Calling" in the prose of the read chapters, but functionally present:
Carden and Renee are both called "Idealist Arcanist" (Ch2) / "Ideals Arcanist" (Epilogue), implying
a Calling tied to
Zeal Magic. See `magic-system.md`.

**Calling-specific feat** — the specific act an Arcanist or Profaner must accomplish at certain
Tiers to progress to the next Tier.
→ Not depicted on-page; no character is shown attempting or completing one in the read chapters.

**Creator** — one of the deities in Eyael's pantheon of gods.
→ Patron deity of Imperium Divinus; His Scripture appears mysteriously in Parliament demanding
submission (Ch5). Secretly worshipped within Alfainia by the Oakmoores (hidden shrine, Ch4) and,
through their 25-year conversion effort, the Winstons. See `magic-system.md` → "Pantheon."

**Demigod** — an Arcanist or Profaner who has reached a high enough Tier to attain divinity.
→ Alfainia maintains resident Demigods seated in Parliament's balconies; a crushing wave of divinity
(attributed to the God of War Himself, not any Demigod) staggers all of them simultaneously at the
Ch1 investiture. Imperial Demigods are credited with the Atmospheric Magic-conjured storms that
screen Imperial fleet withdrawals (Ch3; Ch5 mentions the storm but does not attribute it to
Demigods specifically).

**Dwarves** — short, humanoids with human features and a lack of biological pigmentation as part of
their subterranean evolution.
→ Party to the Mankind/Dwarfkind compact reenacted at every investiture (Ch1); represented by Lord
Magi Feroz Surena.

**Elves** — human-sized humanoids with human features but pointed ears, grey skin, and eye and hair
pigmentation whose color reflects innate individual Magical affinity.
→ Every Elf shown on-page in the read chapters appears in an enslaved role: a riot suspect's
enslaved Elf mother bears a visible slave-tattoo (Ch3); an enslaved Elf practices Mitotic Magic as a
healer at the Exchequer infirmary (Ch5); enslaved Elves (orange-haired and sky-blue-haired) perform
skilled construction labor via Empowerment and Enchanting Magic in the Epilogue, loaned by Windbury's
affluent families under social pressure from Horace. The text notes this detail without yet
interrogating it further — see `magic-system.md` → "Peoples."

**Essence** — an amorphous, gelatinous, indestructible organ in Arcane Creatures, Beastmen, and
Profane Creatures that contains the host-creature's innate Magic.
→ Not shown being harvested or forged on-page; referenced only via its downstream products (Magic
Artifacts/Charms) existing in the world.

**Evania** — an island offshore the Nordic continent and Alfainia's core homeland.
→ Implicit throughout as Alfainia's home island; two of its three regions are named directly via
telegram addresses in Ch3: Vastnir (Marelyn's address) and Ostnir (Carden's address). Nordnir
appears only in the glossary.

**Eyael** — the world.
→ The setting's name; not emphasized in-dialogue in the read chapters (characters say "the world"
rather than "Eyael"), consistent with Eyael functioning as an out-of-universe/glossary-level term.

**Father** — one of the deities in Eyael's pantheon of gods.
→ Carden's (and originally the Winstons') faith; the novella's emotional and thematic throughline,
culminating in Carden's Ch7 cathedral experience and resignation on principle "because the Father
teaches us love is the greatest commandment." See `magic-system.md` → "Pantheon" and
`characters.md` → "Alfred."

**God of War** — one of the deities in Eyael's pantheon of gods.
→ Patron deity of Sarh and, via the liberation-era compact, of Alfainia's government; His divine
presence manifests at the Ch1 investiture (a crushing force, not a physical form); His Scripture
(Ch1) calls for war against the Creator. See
`magic-system.md` → "Pantheon."

**Humans** — self-explanatory.
→ One of the three sapient peoples shown on-page, alongside Dwarves and Elves; party to the
investiture compact (Ch1).

**Magic Artifact** — an instrument forged with an Essence and/or Magic Gland that can cast the
donor-organ's innate Magic.
→ Shown on-page once: Julian's Shadow Magic ring, which opens passage into the Shadow World (Ch5).

**Magic Charm** — a charm imbued with the power of extremely powerful Arcanists or Profaners that
functions as a single-use Magic Artifact.
→ Not shown on-page in the read chapters.

**Magic Gland** — a gelatinous organ in Arcane Creatures that lets them cast Magic.
→ Not shown on-page; referenced only via the glossary's own cross-reference to Magic Artifacts.

**Norden** — the northernmost continent in the world of Eyael.
→ Implied to be where Imperium Divinus's "remaining territory on the mainland" lies, i.e., the
war's physical front (Ch1, Ch5), though not named directly as "Norden" in the dialogue itself.

**Nordnir** — Evania island's northern region: predominantly plateaus and mountains.
→ Named only in the glossary; not referenced directly in the read chapters' prose.

**Ostnir** — Evania island's eastern region, predominantly flatlands.
→ Windbury's home province; named explicitly in Carden's Ch3 telegram address ("Windbury Central
Telegram Office, Ostnir Province").

**Pértus** — the central and largest continent in the world of Eyael.
→ Named only in the glossary as Sarh's continent; not referenced directly by name in the read
chapters' prose.

**Portsmouth** — Alfainia's capital.
→ Primary setting for Chapters 1, 2 (partially), 5, 6, and 7; see `locations.md`.

**Profane** — Eyael's demonic, unorthodox Magic system.
→ Referenced only obliquely, as an idiom ("Profane-infested hells," Ch4) and via the existence of
Profane Creatures/Floods/Tongue/Profaners in the glossary; no Profaner or Profane Magic is shown
actively practiced on-page in the read chapters.

**Profane Creature** — feral biological organisms naturally constituted from Aether during Profane
Floods, innately attuned to the Profane and its Magics, and an aberrant category of invasive
creatures in Eyael's ecosystems.
→ Not depicted on-page.

**Profane Floods** — cyclical, recurrent worldwide catastrophes that inundate the world in Aether
and spawn millions upon billions of Profane Creatures.
→ Not referenced or depicted beyond the glossary definition itself.

**Profane Tongue** — the Profane's mystical language intelligible only to Profaners.
→ Not depicted on-page.

**Profaner** — a person with a soul attuned to the Profane and thus its practitioner.
→ No character is identified as a Profaner in the read chapters.

**Sarh** — a continental empire in the Pértusian continent and one of Eyael's superpowers.
→ Alfainia's patron/ally via the God of War compact; Sarhese "priest-cum-diplomats" are referenced
pointedly at the Ch1 investiture; the Sarhese Eighteenth Aerial Fleet (Commander Shapur Mihran)
shadows the Imperial flotilla off Windbury in Ch3.

**Self-actualization** — a lived, experiential process where an Arcanist or Profaner must embody and
live the ideals of their Calling to progress it and thus ascend to the next Tier's Calling.
→ Gives the Epilogue its title ("Magic Is Self-Actualization") and is the implicit mechanism behind
Carden's visibly surer, calmer command of Zeal Magic after his Ch7 resignation — power following
from lived conviction rather than preceding it. See `magic-system.md`.

**Spawning** — the Aetheric phenomena wherein a Worldheart's Aether coalesces into an Aetheric
Creature, Arcane Creature, or Beastman.
→ Not depicted on-page; referenced only via the glossary's own definition.

**Tier** — the discrete levels of power for Aetheric Creatures, Arcane Creatures, Arcanists,
Beastmen, Profane Creatures, and Profaners.
→ Used directly in Ch2 (Carden recognizes Renee as "at least the same Tier as him") and Ch5 (Julian
stands "a whole Tier above" Carden).

**Windbury** — a major Alfainian city and Ostnir's provincial capital.
→ Primary setting for Chapters 2 (partially)–4, 8, and the Epilogue; see `locations.md`. (Ch6 is
set entirely in Portsmouth.)

**Worldheart** — Naturally-occurring, recurrent, spontaneous natural phenomena where atmospheric
Aether converges into a sky-piercing pillar of light that spawns Aetheric Creatures, Arcane
Creatures, Beastmen, and natural resources.
→ Not depicted on-page; referenced only via the glossary's own definition and its role in the
Spawning process.

**Vastnir** — Evania island's western region, predominantly hills and bays.
→ Portsmouth's home province; named explicitly in Marelyn's Ch3 telegram address ("24 Veronica
Lane... Portsmouth, Vastnir Province").

## Terms used in the manuscript but absent from the author's own glossary

These appear and matter in the read chapters but have no entry in `manuscript/glossary.md`; flagged
here as candidates for the author to add if the glossary is expanded in a later draft.

- **Zeal Magic**, **Law Magic**, **Armaments Magic**, **Shadow Magic**, **Mitotic Magic**,
  **Atmospheric Magic**, **Gravitational Magic**, **Space Magic**, **Empowerment Magic**,
  **Enchanting Magic** — named Magic Branches/disciplines shown in active use (see
  `magic-system.md` for the full table of who wields what, and where).
- **Imperium Divinus** — the Creator's empire; the Latin-style proper name first appears in Ch5
  ("Imperium Divinus committed during its nine-year occupation of the island"). Ch1's Scripture
  references "the Creator and his empire" generically. Implied throughout as Alfainia's historical
  occupier and the war's opposing power.
- **Ideals Arcanist** / **Idealist Arcanist** — the specific Calling shared by Carden Oakmoore and
  Renee Rose; appears as "Idealist Arcanist" (Ch2) and "Ideals Arcanist" (Epilogue). Functions as
  the in-text name for what the glossary's abstract "Calling" system defines.
- **Black Hounds** — the 309th Infantry Regiment / 8th Infantry Division's nickname and matching
  wrist-tattoo insignia (a snarling black hound), shared by Carden and Horace Livingstone; later also
  given to Renee Rose upon her apparent recruitment (Epilogue).
- **Blue Kingfishers** — the Fifth Marine Regiment's nickname and tattoo insignia, Roger Baker's old
  unit.
- **Essexian Church** — the institution/rite associated with Saint Alfred's teachings, named when
  Carden bows "in the Essexian Church's rite" at the Ch7 cathedral.
- **cone** — Alfainia's currency denomination (e.g., "a brass-colored ten-cone bill," Ch3; "a
  gut-churning twelve-hundred-cone expense," Ch4).
- **Scripture** — divine documents from the Father, God of War, and Creator; central plot devices in
  Ch1, Ch5, and Ch7. Italicized as a proper term throughout the manuscript.
- **Deliverance Day** — Alfainia's major religious holiday; central to Ch2–Ch3 events ("Deliverance
  Day normally served as a synonym for festive holiday cheer," Ch2).
- **Dominions** — Alfainia's overseas territories on all seven continents; geopolitically crucial
  throughout (Ch2, Ch5).
- **Saint Alfred** / **Alfred Essex** — Alfainia's founder, liberator, and religious figure; named
  in Ch4, Ch6, Ch7. See `characters.md` → "Alfred."
- **Angels** — referenced as a distinct supernatural category alongside Demigods ("Demigods and
  Angels dueled in displays that warped and cracked the sky," Ch1).
- **Commune** — rural settlements/villages protected by palisades against Aetheric Beast and Arcane
  Beast depredations (Ch2).
- **Arcane Beast** / **Aetheric Beast** — terms used in Ch2 for creatures threatening rural
  Communes; distinct from the glossary's "Arcane Creature" and "Aetheric Creatures."
